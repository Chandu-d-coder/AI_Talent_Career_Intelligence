# Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze the global AI workforce and transform raw employment data into actionable business intelligence.

The project focuses on six major business domains:

- Salary Intelligence
- Career Growth Intelligence
- Workforce Risk Intelligence
- Talent Market Intelligence
- Employee Experience Intelligence
- Recruitment Intelligence

The final outcome is an executive-level report with strategic recommendations for workforce planning and talent management.

---

# Business Problem

Organizations often struggle to answer questions such as:

- What drives AI salaries?
- Which AI skills are most valuable?
- How can employee retention be improved?
- Which countries have the strongest AI hiring demand?
- What factors improve recruitment success?

This project answers these questions using data analytics.

# Key Business Insights

- Experience is the strongest driver of salary growth.
- Salary alone does not guarantee employee satisfaction.
- Layoff Risk influences workforce stability more than Automation Risk.
- Emerging AI domains continue to dominate hiring demand.
- Recruitment success depends on multiple organizational factors rather than compensation alone.
